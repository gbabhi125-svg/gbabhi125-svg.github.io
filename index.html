REACH
Fair-Price Platform for Informal Waste Collection
4

2
GST Slabs
Live
Pricing Engine
A pricing and quota platform that connects waste pickers and kabadiwalas directly to corporate and municipal buyers, replacing exploitative middleman pricing with transparent, auditable rates. A pricing service computes real-time per-kg rates across plastic, metal, paper and e-waste, calibrated against city-level reference data. A quota engine tracks weight thresholds per material and routes surplus to the right buyer — bulk recycler vs. local dealer — instead of just the nearest one. Invoicing is GST-slab aware, reflecting the September 2025 revision (18% on metal/plastic/rubber scrap, 5% on paper). Built to give informal collectors a documented transaction history they can eventually use for microloans or insurance eligibility.
